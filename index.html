🛡️ Before Isaac buys

  • Meet in public for local deals. Buffalo Police run a safe-exchange spot — or meet inside a busy store. Bring someone. Never go to a home address.
  • Test before paying. Power on, check the disc drive (if disc model), confirm it isn't account-locked, and that it factory-resets clean. A controller and cables should be included.
  • Cash or in-app only. For pickup, cash. Online, pay inside eBay/Swappa/Mercari so you're protected. Never Zelle/Venmo/CashApp/gift cards to a stranger — that's the #1 scam.
  • Check the serial isn't reported stolen. Ask for a photo of the serial; be wary of "no box, no cables, must sell today" at a too-low price.
  • Sanity-check the price against the table up top and eBay sold listings. If it's way below "Great," assume it's a scam until proven otherwise.
  • Sell the Series S first or alongside — same marketplaces. A clean Series S typically funds a big chunk of a used PS5 Digital.
